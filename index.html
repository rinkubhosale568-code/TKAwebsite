We do not use the word guaranteed. We support you until you are placed — resume, mocks, company connects, and coaching. Over 96% of students who complete the course and placement prep get placed within 3 months.

4 months for Testing and Data Analytics, 5 months for MERN, 6 months for Java, Python, and Data Science. Placement prep is included.

Both. Classroom at 4 branches, or the same live batch online. Trainer, syllabus, and placement support stay the same.
